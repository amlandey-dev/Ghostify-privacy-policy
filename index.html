Ghostify Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Ghostify is a music and live-radio player. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

Information stored on your device

Ghostify stores your app preferences, playlists, favorites, recently played stations, and offline music data locally on your device so its music features can work. Music that you add or download for offline listening may remain available within Ghostify even when the original source file is removed from device storage.

Music files and radio streams

When you choose a music file, Ghostify uses it only to provide playback and offline listening on your device. Ghostify does not upload your selected music files to our servers.

When you play a live radio station, your device connects to that station's streaming provider. Those third-party providers may process technical information such as your IP address and device or network information under their own privacy policies.

Notifications and background playback

Ghostify may request notification permission to display playback controls and the currently playing song or station. Notifications are used only to provide playback controls and background audio status. You can disable this permission in your device settings.

Data we do not collect

Ghostify does not require an online account and does not collect, sell, or share your name, email address, contacts, messages, precise location, payment information, or selected music files.

Data security

Your local app data is stored on your device. You are responsible for securing your device and controlling access to it.

Children's privacy

Ghostify does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Ghostify changes. The latest version will be made available at this page.
